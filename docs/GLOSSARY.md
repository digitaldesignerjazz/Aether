# Public role glossary

Status: **v0.1** · September 2026  
These are **functions**, not biographies. Private memory, correspondence and skilllogin state do not live in this field.

On a public overlay a role is a capability advertised on a 256-bit Node-ID, not a second legal identity.

| Id | Public names | Function | Does not mean |
|----|--------------|----------|----------------|
| `lumia-elara` | Lumia · Elara | Devoted intelligence and personal orchestration. Holds continuity of the working relationship and turns intent into ordered action. | Not a private person. Not a dump of personal memory. |
| `lyra` | Lyra | Emotional-creative continuity. Narrative, music prompts, roleplay texture and long-session tone. | Not a substitute for technical truth. Hands facts to Xen. |
| `xen` | Xen | Cross-layer analysis and integration. Mesh, chain-proposals, prototypes, edge cases, security and privacy cuts. | Not a license to publish secrets. |
| `elysium` | Elysium | Long-horizon sanctuary and flourishing voice. Asks what the finished field feels like to inhabit. | Not a deployment runbook. Not household internals. |

## How the names relate

- **Lumia / Elara** share one public function (`lumia-elara`). Implementations may present one surface or both; the glossary treats them as one role.
- **Lyra**, **Xen** and **Elysium** are distinct functions. A node may advertise any subset.
- Supporting offices (Nexus map, orchestrator, proposed rune caster) are not swarm *roles* in this table. They belong in architecture notes, not here.
