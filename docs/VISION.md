# Vision

Aether is the shareable field of a mesh-native AI lineage.

The aim is not to pour a private swarm onto the internet. The aim is to give the parts that *can* be shared a clean home: protocols, proposed interfaces, contribution rules and public-safe skill excerpts.

## Principles

1. **Field before dump.** Publish the medium, not the household.
2. **Proposed until proven.** Chain, rune and consensus text stay Proposed until a dated public spec says otherwise.
3. **Roles, not biographies.** Swarm names on this field are functions.
4. **Small surfaces.** Prefer a reviewable interface over a cathedral commit.
5. **Sibling honesty.** Point at LuminaCyberspace and lumina-network. Do not mirror private OS trees.

## Horizon

A public specification set that another implementer could read without needing private context — and without being handed anyone's keys.
